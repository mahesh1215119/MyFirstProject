  1. HeartBeat
    This is a widely-watched Indian medical drama set around the fictional RK Multispeciality Hospital. It chronicles the emotional, professional, and personal struggles of a dedicated team of doctors, interns, and nurses.
    • Streaming Platform: Available on Hotstar (Streamed in Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, and Malayalam).

    • Key Cast: Deepa Balu (as Reena), Anumol K. Manoharan (as Radhi), Charukesh (as Arjun), and Karthik Kumar (as Vijay).


  2. Resort
    This culinary workplace comedy sitcom became a massive hit on JioHotstar. It consists of 100 episodes (with an additional behind-the-scenes special) and concluded its first season on August 28, 2026.
    • Streaming Platform: Available on Hotstar (Streamed in Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, and Malayalam).
    • The story follows Vetri, a 12th-fail village boy carrying heavy family debt, who joins the luxurious RR Resort as a room service worker. Gifted with a natural talent for cooking, Vetri dreams of becoming a professional chef. However, he must survive the strict, dictatorial supervision of the ruthless Executive Chef, Nedumaran.
    • Key Cast: Vijay Kumar Rajendran (as Vettri), Abeneya Nethrun (as Anu), and Thalaivasal Vijay (as Chef Ravi).


  3. Resort
    The plot follows Yazhini, a brilliant young lawyer who struggles with a stammer and lives under the shadow of her famous lawyer father, Raj Sekar. She is eventually forced to face her father in a high-stakes courtroom battle.
    • Streaming Platform: Available on Hotstar (Streamed in Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, and Malayalam).
    • She lives under the shadow of her famous father, Raj Sekar, a top legal expert. Matters turn personal when a major case forces Yazhini to face her father directly in the courtroom.
    • The series explores self-doubt, family tensions, social prejudice, and her fight to prove her capability.
